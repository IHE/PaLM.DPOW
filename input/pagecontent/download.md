> **Note:** DPOW is an HL7 v2.5.1 messaging profile. It defines no FHIR profiles, capability statements, or examples, so the definition- and example-resource downloads offered by other IHE guides do not apply here. Its only FHIR artifacts are the DPOW case-status CodeSystem and ValueSet, included in the complete-guide download above. The normative content is the HL7 v2 message and segment specifications in [Volume 2](LAB-100.html) and the [Volume 2 Appendices](volume-2-appendix.html).
